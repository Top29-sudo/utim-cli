## Season Details

### Summer (March-May)
- **Climate**: Arid to semi-arid
- **Humidity**: Very low (30-40%)
- **UV Index**: High
- **Daylight**: 12-13 hours
- **Food**: Cooling foods like lassi, chaas, watermelon
- **Cultural Events**: Holi, Diwali preparations

### Monsoon (June-September)
- **Climate**: Tropical humid
- **Humidity**: Very high (70-90%)
- **UV Index**: Reduced due to cloud cover
- **Daylight**: 11-12 hours
- **Food**: Warming spices, soups, hot teas
- **Cultural Events**: Ganesh Chaturthi, Diwali celebrations

### Winter (December-February)
- **Climate**: Temperate
- **Humidity**: Moderate (50-60%)
- **UV Index**: Moderate
- **Daylight**: 10-11 hours
- **Food**: Warm dishes, ghee-based dishes, hot beverages
- **Cultural Events**: Christmas, Lohri, Makar Sankranti

## Regional Variations

| Region | Summer Characteristics | Monsoon Characteristics | Winter Characteristics |
|--------|------------------------|-------------------------|------------------------|
| **Northern** | Extreme heat, dust storms | Heavy rainfall, flash floods | Cold, snow in mountains |
| **Southern** | Humid heat, high humidity | Moderate rainfall | Pleasant, mild colds |
| **Western** | Coastal heat, sea breezes | Heavy coastal rains | Cool, monsoon influence |
| **Eastern** | Humid heat, cyclones | Torrential rains, floods | Mild, comfortable |

## Comparative Analysis

### Temperature Comparison (Average °C)
- **Delhi**: Summer 35°, Monsoon 30°, Winter 15°
- **Mumbai**: Summer 32°, Monsoon 28°, Winter 25°
- **Shimla**: Summer 25°, Monsoon 22°, Winter 8°

### Economic Impact
- **Summer**: Increased demand for cooling, air conditioning, tourism to hill stations
- **Monsoon**: Agricultural income boost, infrastructure challenges, flood tourism
- **Winter**: Tourism boom, warm clothing industry, festival economy

### Clothing Recommendations
- **Summer**: Lightweight cotton, UV protection
- **Monsoon**: Waterproof gear, quick-dry fabrics
- **Winter**: Layered clothing, sweaters, warm accessories

## Fun Facts
- **Longest Monsoon**: Kerala (June-September, 120+ days)
- **Shortest Winter**: Southern coastal areas (2-3 months)
- **Hottest Temperature**: India recorded 50°C in Death Valley, Punjab (2010)
- **Highest Winter Rainfall**: Western Ghats receive up to 4000mm annually

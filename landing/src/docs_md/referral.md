# Referral Program

The Referral Page allows you to earn rewards by inviting other developers to UTIM.

## How It Works
- **Share Your Link:** Every user receives a unique referral tracking link.
- **Rewards:** When a user signs up using your link and subscribes to a paid tier, both you and the referred user receive account credits.
- **Tracking:** The Referral dashboard tracks clicks, successful signups, and total credits earned over time.

Credits can be automatically applied to your next billing cycle to reduce your subscription cost.

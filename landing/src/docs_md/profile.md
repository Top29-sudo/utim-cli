# User Profile & Dashboard

The Profile Page is the central dashboard for managing your UTIM account and usage.

## Features
- **Account Details:** View your current plan (Free, Hobby, Pro, Max, Ultimate), email address, and account creation date.
- **Quota Usage:** Track your current billing cycle's AI model usage and credit consumption.
- **Billing Management:** Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your subscription plan easily via the integrated billing portal.
- **Active Devices:** Manage and revoke active UTIM CLI devices linked to your account.
- **API Keys:** Generate and manage personal UTIM API keys if you prefer to bypass device activation and authenticate headless CI/CD systems.

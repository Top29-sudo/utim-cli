# Refund Policy
*Last Updated: February 10, 2026*

This Refund Policy outlines the terms and conditions for credit purchases and premium plan subscriptions made through UTIM.

## 1. Credit Top-Ups (Pay-As-You-Go)
- **Refundable Credits**: Only **unused purchased credits** remaining in your active balance are eligible for a refund. 
- **Example**: If you purchase $10 in credits and consume $3, only the remaining $7 is eligible for a refund.
- **Non-Refundable Credits**: Credits consumed by premium model completions (Claude, GPT-4, Gemini Pro, etc.) are **permanently non-refundable** once processed.

---

## 2. Subscription Plans (Hobby, Pro, Max & Ultimate)
- **7-Day Money-Back Guarantee**: Subscription payments for Hobby ($7.00/mo), Pro ($25.00/mo), Max ($55.00/mo), and Ultimate ($110.00/mo) plans are subject to a full refund within **7 days of purchase**, provided that:
  1. The billing cycle is the initial purchase (renewals are not eligible).
  2. The total usage during the first 7 days has not exceeded 10 premium prompts.
- **Credit Deductions**: If premium credits were bundled with your plan and used, their market value will be deducted from the refund amount.

---

## 3. General Terms
- **Free/Signup Credits**: The $5.00 free credit balance given upon signup has no monetary value and is **strictly non-refundable**.
- **Suspended Accounts**: Accounts suspended or permanently banned for violating our Terms of Service (ToS) are **not eligible** for refunds.
- **Cryptocurrency Payments**: Refunds for payments made via cryptocurrency will be processed at the USD value at the time of refund.

---

## 4. How to Claim a Refund
To request a refund, please email us with:
1. Your account email address.
2. The invoice number or transaction ID.
3. A brief reason for the request.

Send claims to:
- **Email**: support@utim.dev or uthinkimake.official@gmail.com
- **Response Time**: We process and respond to claims within **3 to 5 business days**. Approved refunds take 5-10 business days to clear.
